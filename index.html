Section 80G Benefits

Save Income Tax While Helping Society

Under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961, all donations made to Yuva Chaitanya Charitable Trust are eligible for 50% Tax Deduction.

Why Donate to YCC Trust?

  • 50% Deduction on Taxable Income: Reduce your taxable income by half of the donated amount.
  • Instant Official 80G Receipt: Download fully formatted 80G receipt with PAN details immediately after payment.
  • Valid for Individuals & Corporates: Salaried employees, self-employed individuals, and companies can claim benefits.
  • 100% Transparency & Direct Impact: Trust Regd. No. 278/2017 registered in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.
